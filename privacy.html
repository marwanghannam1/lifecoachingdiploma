Privacy policy

How the Life Coaching Diploma uses your personal information, in plain English.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

In short

  • You can watch the videos and download the workbooks without giving us any personal information.
  • This website does not set its own cookies, and does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • We only collect information you choose to give us: in the exam, the feedback form, the booking form, by email, or when you join our community.
  • We never sell your information.

Who we are

The Life Coaching Diploma (lifecoachingdiploma.com) is provided by Diet Specialist, a member of The CPD Certification Service (member no. 7085), and taught by Marwan Ghannam. We are responsible for (the “data controller” of) the personal information described here. Contact us about privacy at info@haqacademyhub.com.

What we collect and why

  • The exam: your name, email address, answers and score. We use these to mark your assessment, send your certificate and keep a record of the certificates we issue, so that we, you or The CPD Certification Service can check a certificate later.
  • Your 40 Day Immersion: the emails you send us and the work you share. We use these to give you instructions, assess your work and support you.
  • Your certificate: your name, completion date and certificate number.
  • The feedback form: your ratings and comments, and your name and email if you choose to give them. We use feedback to improve the course. We only quote you as a testimonial if you say we may, and in the way you choose.
  • The booking form (meeting us in person): the details you enter, such as your name and contact details, so we can arrange your visit.
  • Emails and messages you send us: so we can reply and help you.

Our lawful basis

We use your information to provide the course and assessment you have asked for (a contract), for our legitimate interests in running and improving the course and keeping reliable certificate records, and with your consent where we ask for it (for example, testimonials). You can withdraw consent at any time.

Services that handle information for us

  • Google Forms (Google): the exam, feedback and booking forms. Your answers are stored in our Google account.
  • Google Fonts (Google): the website’s fonts are loaded from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Vimeo: the course videos. A video only loads when you press play, and we ask Vimeo not to track you.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub): hosts this website, and may keep basic technical logs such as IP addresses for security.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) and Skool: our communities. If you join, the community admins can see your number or profile, but other members cannot see your number. These services have their own privacy policies.
  • Email: messages you send us are held by our email provider.

Some of these companies may store information outside the UK, for example in the USA. They use legal safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies of its own. Google Forms and Vimeo may set their own cookies when you use a form or play a video.

Who we share it with

Only the services listed above, The CPD Certification Service if they ask to check our certificate records as part of our accreditation, and anyone we are required to share it with by law. We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

  • Certificate and assessment records: up to 6 years after you finish, so certificates can be checked.
  • Feedback: up to 3 years, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Booking details and emails: up to 2 years after our last contact.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to: see a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, object to how we use it, or send it to you in a portable format. To do any of these, email info@haqacademyhub.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if anything changes, and show the date it was last updated at the top.
