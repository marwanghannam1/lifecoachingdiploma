Why we want to meet you in person

Our nutrition courses have been taken by tens of thousands of people online, and we have met almost none of them. We don't want that for life coaching.

Life coaching is about helping people become better people, and that matters to us more than numbers. Before we put our name on someone as a life coach, we want to spend a day with them. Is this for you?

You're in the right place if you want to help other people, or if you want help with your own life.

  • You're unsure whether your career has meaning, or which direction to take
  • You're struggling with your health, or you simply lack direction in life

Questions people ask

Is the course itself free?

Yes. All five modules, the workbooks and the meditations are free at lifecoachingdiploma.com, with no login. You only pay to come to the live event.

Do I have to come to get the accredited diploma?

Yes. To receive the CPD accredited diploma you need to pass the online exam and come to the event. If you only want the course for your own benefit, you can do it all online for free.

Is there an exam on the day?

No. The exam is online at lifecoachingdiploma.com/exam. Finish the modules and pass the exam before 23 January.

What does the £59 cover?

Your place for the day, a three-course meal and refreshments. It is the whole cost. Joining Foyht or taking out insurance is separate, and you pay those organisations directly.

Why does the checkout say SIEA Ltd?

Payments go to SIEA Ltd, a charity. The fee only covers the venue and food.

Can I get a refund?

No, places are non-refundable because we confirm numbers with the venue and pay in advance. That is also why bookings close on 18 December 2026.

How much time does the course take?

About an hour or two a week. The modules are short and not difficult, and there is plenty of time before the event.

Can I do life coaching as a business?

Yes. Module 5 shows you how to start coaching others and find clients, and we will talk about it in more detail on the day.

Will this run again?

This is the first time we have run the diploma as an in-person event, and there are no other dates planned. Please assume it will not run again soon.
