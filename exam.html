Your assessment

To receive your CPD accredited Life Coaching Diploma, pass the 30 question exam below and attend the Life Coaching Mastermind, our in-person event in Solihull on Saturday 23 January 2027.

CPD accredited · Recognised by Foyht · Graduates can get insurance through BGi

The exam

30 multiple choice questions, 1 point each. Allow about 30 minutes. Enter your name and email, answer every question, then press Submit to see your score straight away.

Pass mark: 70% (21 out of 30)

Pass the exam before 23 January 2027, then attend the in-person event to receive your CPD accredited Life Coaching Diploma (8 CPD hours).

Did not pass?

Go back over the modules and workbooks for the topics you found difficult, then resit after at least 24 hours. There is no limit on resits.

Not finished the modules yet? Watch the teaching videos first.
