Your assessment

The Life Coaching Diploma assessment has two parts. Complete both to receive your certificate.

Part 1: the exam

30 multiple choice questions covering all five modules. Take it below.

Part 2: your 40 Day Immersion

40 daily tasks that help people align their life with their values. Email us first for instructions. See how.

Part 1: the exam

30 multiple choice questions, 1 point each. Allow about 30 minutes. Enter your name and email, answer every question, then press Submit to see your score straight away.

Pass mark: 70% (21 out of 30)

Pass the exam and complete your 40 Day Immersion, and we will email your Life Coaching Diploma certificate within 7 days.

Did not pass?

Go back over the modules and workbooks for the topics you found difficult, then resit after at least 24 hours. There is no limit on resits.

Not finished the modules yet? Watch the teaching videos first.

Exam not showing? Open the exam in a new tab.

Part 2: produce your own 40 Day Immersion

As part of the assessment, you will produce a 40 Day Immersion: a list of 40 daily tasks that people practise, one task per day, to help them align their life with their values.

Before you start: email us first

Please email info@haqacademyhub.com before you begin. We will send you full instructions on exactly what to do. Please wait for these instructions before you start your 40 Day Immersion.
