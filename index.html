How the diploma works

Life coaching helps people identify and achieve their goals in life. You start by coaching yourself.

  1. Watch a moduleEach one is a recorded session of 25 to 40 minutes. Take them in order.
  2. Do the workbookDownload the PDF for the module and complete the homework.
  3. Practise the meditationShort guided meditations help each lesson sink in.
  4. Repeat for 30 daysChange comes from resistance and repetition, so the trackers last a month.
  5. Coach othersModule 5 shows you how to start helping people and build a community.
  6. Launch your careerMeet us in person in Solihull, or complete a 40 Day Practical Immersion.
