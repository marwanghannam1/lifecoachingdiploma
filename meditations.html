Meditations

Allocate some time during the day to practise these meditations. They are also inside the teaching modules, but are here on their own so they are easy to find.

Goes with Module 1: Your Values4 min

Values Meditation

When in nature, we feel relaxed and happy because the world is based on values such as peace, mercy and love. Watch the scenes and notice the values in each one.

Goes with Module 2: Your Identity2 min 30 sec

Identity Meditation

Mountains, the breath, the eyes, the mind, a beating heart and the people we love. Appreciate each part of you: body, mind, heart and emotions.

Goes with Module 4: Life Alignment11 min

Peace Meditation

A spoken, guided meditation: imagine a light of peace, breathe it in, and let go of stress and overthinking. Learn the method here, then use it every hour, as in Module 4.
